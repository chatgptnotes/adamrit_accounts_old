Recording Transactions in Adamrit

Staff guide - Hope Multi-Specialty Hospital and Ayushman Nagpur Hospital - issued 6 Aug 2026. Based on an audit of 2,199 vouchers posted between 6 Jul and 5 Aug 2026.

The golden rules

1. Never type a voucher by hand. Every transaction below has its own tile or screen that posts the accounting automatically. The manual Tally Voucher Entry screen is only for corrections approved by accounts, and every manual voucher is visible in the Day Book without the AUTO badge.
2. Money follows the patient's hospital. Wherever you search a patient, a Hope / Ayushman switch is above the search. The books that receive the entry are always the hospital you searched in - not the login.
3. Every payment is made against an invoice. If there is no invoice on the Expense Bill page, raise it first (it posts its own journal). Part payment is fine: type any amount up to the outstanding; the invoice stays listed until fully paid.
4. Your name is on everything you post. Every tile records who did it, and the Day Book, Account Logs and the voucher edit log show it. Past bills open only with the office PIN.

Who is recorded, and what is locked

User log. Every voucher stores who created it; tiles pass your login automatically, and from 6 Aug the manual screen also records the typist by name (older manual entries said "system" but kept the name in last-modified-by). Edits are tracked in the voucher edit log and the Account Logs page. Register payment stamps (Akshay payouts) can only be written by the payment function - not by hand. PIN lock. Past bills - the Expense Bill pages, Old Bills and View Bill - open only after the office PIN. One unlock lasts until the tab closes. The M.L. Enterprises company is hidden behind the same PIN.

Last-month evidence: 2,199 vouchers - 97.6% posted automatically by tiles and triggers; the 53 hand-typed ones (mostly bank deposits and vendor payments) all have a tile today. If you find a transaction with no tile, report it - do not type it.
